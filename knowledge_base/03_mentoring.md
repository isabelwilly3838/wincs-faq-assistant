## Mentoring programme
The mentoring programme pairs first- and second-year students with final-year or alumni mentors. Mentors and mentees meet at least once a month for one semester.

## How to apply for mentoring
Applications open in the first two weeks of each semester through a form linked in our newsletter. You must be a WinCS member to apply.

## CV reviews
Committee members and alumni offer free CV reviews in November and February. Book a slot via the newsletter link.

## Internships
WinCS does not hire interns directly, but we share internship opportunities from partner companies in the newsletter and on our Discord.
