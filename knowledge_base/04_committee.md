## Committee roles
The committee has a President, Secretary, Treasurer, Events Officer, Outreach Officer and Social Media Officer.

## Elections
Committee elections happen every April. Any member can stand for a role. Voting is online through the Students' Union.

## Volunteering
Members can volunteer at events without being on the committee. Volunteers help with set-up, registration and running workshops. Sign up through the volunteer form in the newsletter.

## Code of conduct
All members must follow the WinCS code of conduct: be respectful, inclusive and kind. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated. Report concerns to the Welfare contact at welfare@example.org; reports are confidential.
