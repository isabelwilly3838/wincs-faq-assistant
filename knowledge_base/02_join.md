# Joining Women in CS @ TUM

## Ways to join
There are three ways to get involved, and you are welcome in all of them: join the WhatsApp community, become an active member, or join the Talent Pool.

## WhatsApp community
Join our WhatsApp community to stay updated on all our events, workshops and socials. Use the "Join the chat" button in the "Join us" section of our website, womenincstum.github.io.

## Becoming an active member
Active members are the team behind the scenes: they organise events, shape our programmes and grow with other motivated women in tech. Applications for active members are currently closed and reopen in April 2027.

## Talent Pool
The Talent Pool lets partner companies reach you with scholarships, programmes, internships and other opportunities for women in tech. Add your details with the "Join the Talent Pool" button in the "Join us" section of our website.
