# Events

## Types of events
We run four kinds of events: social get-togethers, hands-on workshops, career events and industry collaborations, and internal events for our team.

## Finding upcoming events
Upcoming workshops, talks and socials are listed on the Events page of our website (womenincstum.github.io/events), where you can also see past highlights and save your spot. Every event is also announced in our WhatsApp community.

## ITCS Job Fair 2026
We took part in the ITCS Job Fair on 30 October 2026, from 9:30 to 16:00, at the MOC Event Center, Halle 3, Lilienthalallee 40, 80939 München. It was a career event where students could meet tech companies and discover new career paths.
