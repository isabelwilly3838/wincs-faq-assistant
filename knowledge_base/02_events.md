## Weekly meetings
General meetings happen every Wednesday at 18:00 in Room CS-101 during term time. Each meeting lasts about one hour.

## Coding workshops
Beginner coding workshops (Python and web development) run every second Saturday from 10:00 to 13:00. Bring your own laptop; a few loan laptops are available if you email the committee 2 days in advance.

## Hackathon
Our annual hackathon is in March. Teams have 2 to 4 people. Registration opens in January and is free for members.

## Socials
We hold a social event once a month, such as a games night or a coffee meet-up. Socials are alcohol-free.

## Accessibility
All event rooms are step-free. If you need an adjustment (for example captions or a quiet space), email the committee and we will arrange it.
