# Team, contact and partners

## Board
The association is led by a student board. The board roles are Organization & Management, PR & Communication, Social Media & Marketing, and Events & Community.

## Active members
Besides the board, a team of active members volunteers its time to run every workshop, social and partnership.

## Partnering and sponsoring
Companies can partner with us to help power workshops and events for women in tech. They can sponsor an event, host a workshop, or add open roles to our Talent Pool. To become a partner, email womenincstum@gmail.com.
